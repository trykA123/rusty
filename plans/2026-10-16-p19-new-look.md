# P19 · The new look (D41–D45)

Status: ready · Repo: `<repo>` · Branch: `p19-lN` per atom · Started: —

## Goal

Ferrite looks like the designs the owner picked: modern and sleek (2027), Total Commander in behaviour only. The main window uses cards on Mica, the F-key bar is a row of keycaps with Shift, Ctrl and Alt layers, copy progress is a floating card that shrinks into a pill next to F10, the app menu has quick tiles, and settings open as a side sheet with live preview.

## Decisions

All decided; workers never reopen them. Sources: D41, D42, D43, D45 in docs/decisions.md, and the published prototypes:
- Main window and copy card: the design round behind D42.
- Menus and settings: https://claude.ai/artifact/BX7K9xiyp8QZ2Lm4SQZ5jM (D43).
- Viewer and copy pill: https://claude.ai/artifact/CYsfu87k5G27ieXtjiQZty (D45).

- **D1 — Main window (D42).** Direction B: the two panes are cards on a Mica background with an accent ring on the active card; breadcrumb, drive pill and column header inside the card; status footer per card.
- **D2 — F-key bar (D43).** Keycaps in fixed places; holding Shift, Ctrl or Alt lights that cap and switches the layer's labels; the pill area next to F10 is reserved for copy progress.
- **D3 — Copy card (D42, D45).** B's floating job card, opaque; Ctrl+J or the chevron shrinks it into the pill next to F10, the F-keys slide left; several jobs show a count and combined progress; below about 1100 px the pill drops the job name, then the time left.
- **D4 — App menu and settings (D43).** The app button opens a compact Ferrite menu with a theme switch and quick-settings tiles; settings open as an opaque side sheet over the passive pane, so the active pane is the live preview; changes apply live, save to `ferrite.toml`, and Ctrl+Z undoes; picture pickers for theme and density; the focused row shows its TOML key and F3 jumps to it.
- **D5 — Viewer (D45).** Done as P15 A5d.
- **D6 — Tokens first.** New colours, radii, shadows and motion become tokens in the existing theme, light and dark; no hard-coded values; contrast checked.
