# Tiled panes · B6

Status: ready (QT1–QT4 open; architect defaults apply until answered) · Repo: `rusty` · Branch: `beta-b6-<atom>` per atom · Started: 2026-10-27

## Goal

A window shows two, three or four file panes. Ctrl+L picks a layout preset; panes are numbered and keep their folders when the preset changes. Users also split, resize, swap and close panes freely from the keyboard and save their own layouts as presets. Shift+drag on a pane's header swaps it with another pane. F5 and F6 copy and move to a visible target pane.

Sources: D80 7, D81, D82 (owner picks: direction D of `.crew/design/tiling/index.html`, plus free layouts, own presets and Shift+drag swap), beta plan `plans/2026-10-27-beta.md` D8, D9, D14 and Q3, Q5.

## What exists today (checked at `c016ee35`, paths under `crates/`)

- **Core layout tree.** `rusty-core/src/layout/tree.rs:21-29`: `Node::Leaf(PaneId)` or `Node::Split { axis, ratio, first, second }`, ratio in permille (100–900, `tree.rs:4-6`). `Layout` (`layout/mod.rs:9-12`) has `split`, `close`, `focus`, `neighbour`, `focus_direction`, `swap(direction)`, `resize(direction, delta)` and `rects()` (`layout/mod.rs:70-169`). Tests in `layout/tests/unit.rs` (440 lines) and `props.rs`.
- **Core commands.** Only `LayoutSplit`, `LayoutClose`, `LayoutFocus` (`rusty-core/src/command/mod.rs:35-37`), applied in `rusty-core/src/apply/layout.rs:1-24`. `swap` and `resize` have no command. `AppState::split` gives the new pane one tab at the focused tab's folder (`rusty-core/src/state/mod.rs:188-197`).
- **Session.** `LayoutState` (`rusty-core/src/session/mod.rs:35-43`) stores the tree with ratios; `SESSION_VERSION` is 2 (`rusty-store/src/session/mod.rs:13`).
- **UI draws two panes at most.** `panes_view` pops two views into one `Splitter` (`rusty-ui/src/window_view/layout.rs:26-48`). The ratio lives only in a UI `SplitterState` (`window_view/mod.rs:158,208`); the core ratios are never drawn. One-pane mode below 900 px is `shown_panes` (`layout.rs:7-24`).
- **"The other pane" is guessed in four places,** each as "the first pane that is not focused": copy and move (`rusty-ui/src/browser/operate/mod.rs:281-290`), clone tab (`browser/open.rs:85-100`, which picks the first neighbour in any direction), Ctrl+K "other pane" (`window_view/command_center/open.rs:142-147`) and Tab (`window_view/pane.rs:392-400`).
- **Keys in the way.** Ctrl+L is `pane.edit_path` (`rusty-ui/src/commands/keymap.toml:97-98`). Ctrl+1…9 go to tabs (`keymap.toml:45-78`). Ctrl+W closes a tab (`keymap.toml:41-42`). Ctrl+Shift+0 is `pane.reset_split` (`keymap.toml:105-106`). Ctrl+Shift+1…4, Alt+D and Ctrl+Shift+L are free.
- **Settings** are TOML with comment-preserving edits (`rusty-store/src/settings/edit.rs`), live reload, sections in `settings/model.rs:12-28` and `settings/view.rs`. Multi-rename presets live in `navo.db` (`rusty-store/src/db/rename_presets.rs`).

## Decisions

Final for this plan. A worker that finds one wrong stops and reports.

- TD1 — **The core tree is the only layout.** The UI draws every pane from `Layout::rects()` and every divider from the tree. The UI `SplitterState` for panes goes away; ratios live in the core and in the session.
- TD2 — **At most four panes in the beta** (`MAX_PANES = 4` in `rusty-core`). A split beyond four refuses with "Four panes is the most in this version."
- TD3 — **Panes are numbered 1–4 in reading order** of their rectangles (top to bottom, then left to right). Numbers change when the layout changes; folders never do.
- TD4 — **Built-in presets** (keys 1–5 in the switcher): 1 Side by side `row(50, pane, pane)`; 2 Three columns `row(33, pane, row(50, pane, pane))`; 3 Wide + 2 `col(52, pane, row(50, pane, pane))`; 4 Two by two `row(50, col(50, pane, pane), col(50, pane, pane))`; 5 Main + 3 `row(60, pane, col(33, pane, col(50, pane, pane)))`.
- TD5 — **Applying a preset keeps folders.** Pane k (by number) goes to slot k. A bigger preset adds panes at the focused tab's folder. A smaller preset removes the highest-numbered panes; their tabs join the last kept pane (QT3). Focus stays on the same pane if it survives, else on pane 1.
- TD6 — **Ctrl+L opens the layout switcher, which is also layout mode** (QT1). The switcher shows preset thumbnails with their digit and a key bar. Inside it: 1–9 apply a preset; arrows focus a neighbour; Shift+arrows swap with it; V splits right, S splits below; X closes the focused pane; Ctrl+arrows move the nearest divider by 5 %; = makes every split even; Ctrl+S saves the current layout as a preset; Delete removes the selected user preset after a confirmation; Enter or Esc leaves. Path edit moves to Alt+D.
- TD7 — **Direct keys outside the switcher.** Ctrl+Shift+1…4 focus pane n (QT2). Tab toggles between the active pane and the target pane, as in two-pane mode. No Ctrl+Alt keys: they are AltGr on European keyboards.
- TD8 — **The target pane (beta Q5).** The core window keeps `target: Option<PaneId>`: the most recently focused other pane, or pane 1 / pane 2 when none is set. F5, F6, clone tab (Ctrl+Shift+Up), Ctrl+K "other pane" and Tab all use it. With two panes this is the other pane, as today. The target shows a dashed outline and an "F5/F6 → here" tag; the active pane's header is filled with the accent.
- TD9 — **Free sizes.** Dragging a divider sets its ratio (10–90 %); double-click on a divider makes that split even. Ratios save with the session.
- TD10 — **Shift+drag swaps.** Pressing Shift and dragging from a pane's header (tab row or path bar, not the file list, where Shift+drag of files means move) shows the dragged pane's number under the pointer and an accent outline on the pane below. Dropping swaps the two panes' places. Esc or dropping outside cancels.
- TD11 — **User presets live in the settings file.** `[[layout.presets]]` tables with `name` and `tiles` (the TD4 notation), at most 20; the first four take keys 6–9. Why settings, not the session: a preset is a preference shared by every window and workspace; the session is binary state that a reset or a version refusal drops; the owner copies the settings file to another PC and can edit a preset by hand; settings already reload live in every window. Why not `navo.db` like rename presets: layout presets are few, small and readable. A preset stores shape and sizes only, not folders (QT4).
- TD12 — **Narrow windows.** Each pane needs at least 280 × 160 px. Below 900 px window width (beta D8), or when the layout cannot give every pane its minimum, the window shows only the focused pane and a pane strip above it: one button per pane with its number and folder name; the target's button is marked. Tab and Ctrl+Shift+n still work; F5 and F6 show the hidden target's path in their dialog (as B2 L4 does today).
- TD13 — **Workspaces keep their own layout** (beta D14, B4 W1). A workspace snapshot holds the layout tree with ratios, the panes and the target. Presets are global; Ctrl+L changes only the current workspace.
- TD14 — **Motion.** A new pane grows from its seam in 150 ms; a swap cross-fades in 120 ms; both honour reduced motion. Nothing flashes.

### TL1 — Tiles notation

- **Change:** new `rusty-core/src/layout/tiles.rs`. `Tiles` is a tree: `Pane` or `Split { axis: Axis, percent: u8, first, second }`. Grammar: `row(<10–90>, <tiles>, <tiles>)`, `col(…)` and `pane`; spaces are free; `row` is side by side, `col` is stacked. `Tiles::parse` refuses more than `MAX_PANES` (4) leaves, a percent outside 10–90, depth over 8 and any other text, with a `TilesError` that names the column. `Tiles::print` gives the canonical form. `Preset { name: &'static str, tiles: Tiles }` and `Tiles::builtin() -> [Preset; 5]` per TD4. Export from `layout/mod.rs`.
- **Check:** tests: each built-in parses and prints back unchanged; five leaves, `row(95, …)`, `row(50, pane)` and `grid` fail with the right column; whitespace variants parse equal.
- **Stop if:** `Axis` must become public outside `layout`; report the visibility you need.

### TL2 — Layout operations

- **Change:** in `layout/mod.rs` and `tree.rs`: `numbered() -> Vec<PaneId>` in reading order of `rects()` (y, then x, with a 0.001 tolerance); `swap_panes(a, b)` on any two leaves (reuse `Node::exchange`); `SplitPath(Vec<bool>)` (false = first child); `splits() -> Vec<SplitInfo { path, axis, ratio, rect }>`; `set_ratio(&SplitPath, ratio)` clamped to 100–900; `even()` sets every split so leaves share space equally along each axis (a split with 1 leaf against 2 gets 333).
- **Check:** unit tests: numbering of all five TD4 shapes; swap keeps focus on the same pane id; `set_ratio` on a bad path errors; `even()` on `row(70, pane, row(50, pane, pane))` gives 333 then 500; existing layout tests and props stay green.

### TL3 — Apply a preset

- **Change:** `Layout::apply(&mut self, tiles: &Tiles, new_pane: &mut dyn FnMut() -> PaneId) -> Applied { removed: Vec<PaneId>, added: Vec<PaneId> }` and `Layout::to_tiles() -> Tiles`. Pane k of `numbered()` fills leaf k of the template in reading order; extra panes are removed (highest numbers first); missing ones come from `new_pane`. Focus stays if the pane survives, else pane 1.
- **Check:** tests: 2 → 4 panes adds two ids and keeps the old two at numbers 1 and 2; 4 → 2 removes numbers 3 and 4; applying the current shape is a no-op; `to_tiles` then `apply` round-trips every built-in; a property test keeps ids unique.

### TL4 — Window tiling

- **Change:** `Window` gains `target: Option<PaneId>` and keeps it valid (focus change makes the old focused pane the target; a removed target falls back to the lowest-numbered other pane). New methods: `apply_tiles(tiles, make_pane)` moves the tabs of removed panes, in order, to the end of the last kept pane's tabs (TD5); `swap(a, b)`, `set_ratio`, `even`, `focus_number(n)`; `split` refuses past `MAX_PANES` with `StateError::TooManyPanes`. The workspace `Snapshot` (B4 W1) carries `target`, so each workspace keeps its own target. `AppState` gets the matching thin wrappers. New panes get one tab at the focused tab's folder, as `AppState::split` does.
- **Check:** tests in `apply/tests/layout.rs`: 4 → 2 keeps every tab (count before = count after); the target follows focus; closing the target picks another; a fifth split errors with the TD2 text; tab ids stay unique; a workspace switch and back restores the three-pane layout and its target.
- **Stop if:** moving tabs between panes needs a change in `state/pane.rs` beyond one `take_tabs`/`append_tabs` pair; report the shape.

### TL5 — Layout commands

- **Decided 2026-10-27 (TL5 questions):** `split=<path>` is `root` for the root split, else a string of `1` and `2` from the root down (`1` = first child, `2` = second), so `split=21` is the first child of the root's second child.
