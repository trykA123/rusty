Ferrite roadmap

Where we are, in features you can try. Updated 16 October 2026.

Where we are

1 · Works today in the alpha download
  • Two panes with tabsBack and forward. Your session comes back at start.
  • Fast file listHuge folders, filter as you type, sizes in KB to TB, sort by name, extension, date or size.
  • Total Commander style selectionSpace, Insert, right-click, Num+, Num− and Num*.
  • Drive bar and network drives
  • File operationsCopy, move, rename (F2), new folder, Recycle Bin and Shift+Del.
  • Background jobsProgress and cancel. A job cut short is rolled back safely at the next start.
  • F3 viewer for textColours for many languages, hex view, huge files page by page, correct encodings.
  • F4 editor
  • New today: images in F3Zoom, pan, next and previous image, correct rotation.
  • New today: search and marksSearch shows “3 of 1,248”. Find All, colour marks (Ctrl+1 to 8), bookmarks, Follow for growing logs. All kept after restart.
  • Archives in F3Zip, 7z and tar listed with sizes and dates. Filter, and Enter opens an entry.
  • Fonts in F3Sample text in several sizes and a glyph grid.
  • Images: own keys and mouse zoomF3 images show their own keys in the bottom bar. The mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, and drag pans.
  • Live search countThe “x of y” count updates while you type.
  • Stepping through filesSpace and Backspace step files only for images. A held Backspace no longer freezes.
  • Light and dark themesThey follow Windows. F-key bar at the bottom.
2 · Being finished this week
  • Structured files in F3JSON, YAML and XML as a tree, CSV as a table. Done, in the next download.
  • Markdown in F3Rendered in the viewer. In final safety review.
  • Instant Back into foldersListings are cached in memory (64 MB) and refreshed in the background. The cursor is kept, and filter and sort work at once. Done, in the next download.
  • Faster folder listing on Windows3–5× faster in tests, which helps network shares. Done, in the next download.
  • Windows context menu in Ferrite styleDrawn in Ferrite’s style with a type-to-filter list. The menu is built; connecting it to right-click is next.
  • The new lookIn progress: cards on Mica for the panes, and a keycap F-key bar with Shift, Ctrl and Alt layers. Then the copy card with a small mode next to F10, the app menu with quick tiles, and settings as a side sheet with live preview.
  • The new viewer look you pickedFunction name in the header, Ctrl+O list of functions, marks and bookmarks, coloured scrollbar, one-row search with a colour drawer.
  • More colours and encodingsColours for m4, OIL, DCM and Notepad++ user languages. An encoding picker.
3 · Comes next for the alpha
  • Automotive files in F3HEX and S-record memory maps, ELF sections and symbols, linker map flash usage, DBC and A2L signals, CAN traces as tables, ARXML.
  • PDF in F3Chrome’s PDFium engine in a separate process for safety. Pages, zoom, search and copy text.
  • Markdown live previewSplit view while you edit.
  • Spreadsheets in F3Excel and other spreadsheets (xlsx, xls, ods) as a table.
  • Live folders and network sharesFolder changes appear by themselves. Network shares get an “offline, showing cached” state, and pinned folders get a disk cache.
  • Known bug: Follow on WindowsFollow (Alt+F) does not show new lines yet on Windows. Parked for now.
