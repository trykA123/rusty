v1.0 · replaces Total Commander

The release that can stand in for Total Commander.

  • Find filesBy content and attributes, in a pane you can work in.
  • Saved searchesRun a search again.
  • Virtual collectionsA pane holds results or files from many folders.
  • Archives as foldersBrowse, extract and pack zip, 7z and tar.
  • Compare and sync foldersA calm full-window look with a change map.
  • ChecksumsBLAKE3, SHA-256, SHA-1 and MD5.
  • Properties drawer
  • Total Commander keymapFamiliar keys.
