Ferrite preview drawer: four directions (D52)

A live preview of whatever is under the cursor in the active pane, without leaving the panes. Folders show a background size count, counts, last change, types, the 10 largest files and a collapsible tree. Files show the F3 views read-only. The preview follows the cursor after a 150 ms pause, so holding an arrow key loads only where you stop.

Recommended: 1. One drawer component shared with the settings sheet (D43), full height for code, trees and the top-10 list, and a width that does not depend on the pane split.

Keys 1–4 pick a direction. Inside the window: ↑ ↓ Home End move the cursor · Tab switches panes · Q or Ctrl+Q opens and closes · [ and ] resize · Enter or F3 opens · Esc stops a folder scan. Shift+Tab leaves the window.

Direction 1

My recommendation: direction 1

Build the side sheet over the passive pane. It is the drawer the owner asked for, and it is the same component as the settings sheet (D43): one opaque surface, one grip, one footer strip for key hints. Full height suits what a preview mostly shows: code, JSON trees, the 10 largest files and the folder tree. Its width is its own, so a narrow peek or a wide reading view does not move the pane splitter.

Tab keeps working: the sheet jumps to the other side, so it always covers the passive pane and never the cursor it follows. At 1100 px and below it snaps exactly over the passive card, which is what direction 3 does anyway.

Fallbacks. Direction 3 is the cheapest to build (a pane mode, no overlay) and the most familiar to Total Commander users; take it if the sheet's overlap with settings feels crowded. Direction 2 is the only one that keeps both panes visible, but code and images get a short, wide box. Direction 4 keeps everything in view and is the most cramped.
