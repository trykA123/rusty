Modifier hints: what does Ctrl, Shift or Alt do here?

Hold a modifier for 400 ms and Ferrite shows every shortcut on that layer, then hides it on release. A quick tap or a chord such as Ctrl+C never shows it. The F-key bar keeps switching labels as today. Every direction has the same settings row: appearance.modifier_hints.

Keys 1–4 pick a direction. Click the window, then hold Ctrl, Shift or Alt with the real keyboard; add Shift to Ctrl for its layer. While held, try Ctrl+C/X/V, Ctrl+F3–F6, Ctrl+1–3 (the browser may take Ctrl+1–9, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W and Ctrl+Tab), Ctrl+L, Ctrl+, and Alt+←/→. ↑↓ move the cursor, Tab switches panes.

Direction A

Recommendation: A, the drawer, with D's column-header badges as a later extra

Build A. One grouped shelf above the bar shows the whole layer at a glance, grows only as tall as the layer needs (Shift and Alt stay one or two rows), and is the drawer the owner asked about. It covers only the lowest rows, and the active list slides up by transform when the cursor row would be hidden, so nothing reflows.

Why not the others. B never covers the active pane but blanks the other pane, which you are often looking at while copying. C teaches positions well but is the tallest and needs short labels. D is the lightest and teaches where things live, but it scatters the layer and has no home for keys like Ctrl+J or Ctrl+R.
