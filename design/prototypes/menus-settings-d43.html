Ferrite: menus and settings, round 2

Four directions for one system: the app button and its menu, the F-key bar with Shift, Ctrl and Alt layers, and settings as a modern dialog that applies changes live. The mockups are interactive: click categories, toggles and segments, and the panes update. Keys: 1–4 direction, W window, M app menu, S settings. Hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt to see that layer of the F-key bar.

My recommendation

Build A as the base, and take two parts from B and one from D. A's side sheet covers only the passive pane, so the active pane is the live preview with nothing faked. It is opaque, so GPUI can build it now; Mica goes only behind the title bar, where the vendored GPUI already supports it. From B, take the quick-settings tiles in the app menu and the visual pickers for theme and density. From D, take the TOML key on the focused row and the "F3 TOML line" key. Keep A's keycap F-key bar: the lit modifier cap shows the active layer, and every key stays in the same place. Choose C instead if "impressive" matters more than keeping the panes in view. Its live miniature and list of changes look the most 2027.

Token changes against design_b.rs

  • New surfaces: raised for menus and sheets, a lighter step in dark mode to show elevation. chrome and chrome_alt tint the title bar, where Mica shows through. cap and cap_edge draw keycaps. layer_tint marks an active modifier layer. scrim_soft dims behind a sheet. acrylic, card and glass_edge are for B only.
  • Text tokens: ok_text (46%) and warn_text (45%) for light mode. ok and warn stay for icons. Git status letters use the text tokens.
  • Type: tiny 11 → 12 px and key 10.5 → 12 px, to meet the 12 px minimum. New sizes: 16 (sheet titles), 20 and 24 (page titles). C adds Archivo at width 112 for display headings. GPUI has no variable-font axes, so that needs a static font file.
  • Radii: xs 3 merges into sm 4. lg goes from 7 to 8 to match the Windows 11 window corner. New: xl 12 for sheets and the dock, and pill for chips.
  • Elevation and motion: three shadow steps (controls, dock, menus and sheets). Durations 120, 180 and 260 ms. Ease-out on enter, ease-in on leave.
  • Controls: borders use line with a faint bottom edge, Windows 11 style. The bottom edge reaches 3.9:1 in light and 4.2:1 in dark. Every new text pair passes 4.5:1 in both themes (checked with contrast.mjs).

The panes are kept from round 1 on purpose: the main window gets its own design round (D41). Light and dark tokens come from design_b.rs; the new tokens are listed above.
