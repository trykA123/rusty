# Workspaces

Status: in. Workspaces, the tray, drafts, the status-bar button and restore after a restart work.

## What it is

A workspace is a saved arrangement of one window: its panes, tabs, folders and target pane. Keep one workspace per task and switch with Alt+1 to Alt+9. A window holds one to nine workspaces.

Each workspace keeps its own layout, its own target pane and its own tabs. Switching leaves the workspace you left exactly as it was. All of this is kept when you restart Navo.

## What you can do

- Switch to workspace 1 to 9 with Alt+1 to Alt+9.
- Hold Alt in the file list to see the tray. It shows one card per workspace with a small picture of its layout, the folder names, the number and the name. The current workspace says "open".
- Press Alt+1 to Alt+9 with Alt held to preview a workspace. A ribbon says "Previewing <name>". Release Alt to switch. Press Esc to stay where you are.
- Press Alt+N to make a new workspace from the current layout. It opens at once.
- Rename a workspace with F2 or the pencil on its card. A name has 1 to 40 characters.
- Move a workspace left or right with Alt+Shift+Left and Alt+Shift+Right, or with the arrows on its card. Alt+Left and Alt+Right stay Back and Forward.
- Delete a workspace from its card. Nothing asks. Press Ctrl+Z to bring it back to its place. The last workspace cannot be deleted.
- Click the workspace button at the end of the status bar to keep the tray open. The button shows the name, the position such as "2/4" and "Draft" when there are unsaved changes.

## Drafts

Changes you make in a workspace are not saved by themselves. When you switch away, they stay as a draft. The card shows "Draft" and the number of changed panes.

- Ctrl+S saves the draft into the workspace.
- Discard on the card drops the draft and returns to the saved state.

Drafts survive a restart, like everything else in the session.

## Keys

- Alt+1 to Alt+9 switch to a workspace, or preview it while the tray is open.
- Alt+Esc cancels a preview and stays.
- Alt+N makes a new workspace from the current layout.
- F2 renames the workspace on its card.
- Alt+Shift+Left and Alt+Shift+Right move the workspace.
- Ctrl+S saves the draft.
- Ctrl+Z restores a deleted workspace.

The tray does not appear inside a viewer, a dialog or Ctrl+K.
